Why Timeless

Technology changes quickly. Enduring organizations understand which changes matter.

Every few years a technology arrives that is described as the one that changes everything. Some of them do. But most of the value does not accrue to the organizations that adopted earliest — it accrues to the ones that understood soonest what the technology meant for how they should operate. That is the discipline this group is named for.
